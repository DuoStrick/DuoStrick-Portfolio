## How to Play: The Basics

The rules are identical to classic 2048:

1. **Swipe** in any direction (up, down, left, right)
2. All tiles slide in that direction
3. Two tiles with the **same number merge** into one tile with their sum
4. A **new tile (2 or 4)** appears after every swipe
5. **Goal**: keep merging and reach the highest number possible

The infinite grid means new space opens up as you play — but only when you push tiles in that direction. Managing how the grid expands is part of the strategy.

## Beginner Strategy: The Basics That Actually Work

### Anchor Your Highest Tile

Pick one corner — top-left works great — and keep your biggest tile there. This is the foundation of every advanced strategy. Never let your highest tile get stranded in the center.

### Use Only Two or Three Swipe Directions

New players swipe reactively in all four directions. This scatters tiles and creates gaps that are almost impossible to clean up. Discipline yourself to swipe mostly **left and down** (or right and up — pick a corner and commit to it).

### Merge Small Tiles Before They Pile Up

Every 2 and 4 that sits unmerged is a tile blocking your board. Make a habit of clearing small tiles every few moves. This "flush" technique keeps your board clean and gives room for larger merges.

## Intermediate Strategy: Building the Snake

The most reliable path to high scores is the **snake pattern**. Your board should look like this:

```
4096 | 2048 |  512 |  128
2048 |  512 |  128 |   32
 512 |  128 |   32 |    8
 128 |   32 |    8 |    2
```

Every row descends toward the bottom-right. Every column descends downward. Tiles flow toward your highest tile in a continuous chain.

To maintain this pattern, swipe mostly **left** and **down**. Swipe right or up only when you have a specific reason.

## Advanced Strategy: Using the Infinite Grid

Here's where 2048 Puzzle Game Offline differs from all other versions. When your board gets messy, you have an option classic 2048 never gives you: **expand the grid**.

A strategic sideways swipe can open new columns, giving you room to reorganize. This is a powerful escape valve — use it when you're stuck, not as a crutch.

### The Reset Move

When your board looks chaotic and no clean merge is available:

1. Look for a swipe direction that will create at least 2–3 merges
2. If none exists, choose the direction that expands the grid most usefully
3. Never swipe just to swipe — every move should have a purpose

## How to Reach 8192 (and Beyond)

Getting to 8192 requires maintaining the snake pattern for hundreds of moves. The key discipline:

- **Never break the chain** — if a swipe would move your 4096 tile out of its corner, don't do it
- **Build the next tier** — while your 4096 tile sits in the corner, build 2048 → 4096 on the adjacent tile
- **Think two merges ahead** — before every swipe, mentally simulate where tiles will land

Players who reach 8192 are typically holding their highest tile in a corner for the entire game from the moment they hit 1024.
