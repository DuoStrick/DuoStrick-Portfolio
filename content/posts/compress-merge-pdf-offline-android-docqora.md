You need to email a scanned form, but it's 18 MB and the limit is 5. Or a portal wants one PDF and you have four. The quickest fix is usually a search for "compress PDF online" — and a website that asks you to upload the file.

That's fine for a recipe. It's a different matter for a rental agreement, a payslip, a medical report or a scan of your ID. Once a file is uploaded, you're trusting a stranger's server to process it and delete it afterwards.

You can do the same jobs on your phone instead. This guide uses [**Docqora**](/apps/docqora), our free PDF toolkit for Android, which works fully offline.

## Why Offline Matters for PDFs

The documents people most often need to shrink or combine are exactly the ones they'd least like to share: contracts, bank statements, invoices, certificates, ID scans.

Docqora doesn't request Android's internet permission at all. That isn't a privacy setting you have to find — the app has no way to send a file anywhere. Everything below happens on the phone, and it works just as well in airplane mode.

## How to Compress a PDF on Android

1. Open Docqora and tap **Compress**.
2. Pick the PDF from your phone.
3. Choose a strength — **light**, **balanced** or **strong**. Balanced suits most scans; strong is for when you have to get under a hard upload limit.
4. Save the smaller copy. The original stays untouched.

Tip: scanned documents shrink much more than PDFs that are mostly text, because most of their size is the page images.

## How to Merge Several PDFs Into One

1. Tap **Merge** and choose the files.
2. Put them in the order you want.
3. Combine them into a single PDF.

Handy for job and visa applications that want one document, or for bundling a month of receipts.

## How to Split a PDF

Tap **Split** and choose how to cut it:

- **By page ranges** — for example 1–3, 5, 8–10
- **Every N pages** — break a long document into equal chunks
- **Every page separately** — one file per page
- **Selected pages into one file** — pick pages from a grid

## Organise, Rotate and Protect

- **Organise** — reorder, delete, duplicate or insert pages
- **Rotate** — fix sideways or upside-down scans
- **Protect** — add a password before you share a confidential file, or remove one from a file you own

## Convert Between Images and PDF

- **Image to PDF** turns photos of documents (JPG, PNG and more) into a clean PDF
- **PDF to Image** exports pages as JPG or PNG — useful when a form only accepts images

## Quick Checklist Before You Share a Document

- Compress it so it fits the upload or email limit
- Remove pages the recipient doesn't need
- Add a password if it contains personal or financial details
- Send the password through a different channel from the file
