AV Player: Video & MP3 Player is our all-in-one media player for Android. It handles every video and audio format you're likely to encounter — no third-party codecs, no subscriptions, no ads.

This guide walks through every feature in detail so you know exactly what you're getting.

## All Format Video Playback

AV Player supports every major video format:

- **MP4** — the most common format for downloaded videos
- **MKV** — popular for high-quality movies with embedded subtitles
- **AVI** — classic format still used in older media libraries
- **MOV** — Apple's format, fully supported on Android
- **FLV, WMV, 3GP, M4V, WEBM, TS** — complete coverage

The app uses **hardware acceleration** on supported devices, which means 4K and Full HD videos play smoothly even on mid-range phones with minimal battery drain.

## Offline MP3 Music Player

AV Player doubles as a full-featured offline music player:

- **MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, OGG, M4A** — all major audio formats
- **Background playback** — music continues when you switch apps or lock the screen
- **Playlist management** — create and manage playlists directly in the app
- **Folder browsing** — navigate your music by folder structure
- **Lock screen controls** — play, pause, skip without unlocking your phone
- **Notification controls** — manage playback from the notification shade

FLAC support is particularly important for audiophiles — AV Player delivers lossless audio at full quality without downsampling.

## Gesture Controls

One of AV Player's most useful features is gesture-based control:

- **Swipe left/right** on the screen — seek forward or backward
- **Swipe up/down on the left half** — adjust screen brightness
- **Swipe up/down on the right half** — adjust volume

These gestures work in portrait and landscape mode. Once you get used to them, you'll never want to tap tiny buttons again.

## Picture-in-Picture (PiP) Mode

AV Player supports Android's native Picture-in-Picture mode. While watching a video, you can press the home button and the video continues in a small floating window. Perfect for:

- Watching videos while browsing the web
- Following along with a tutorial while using another app
- Keeping an eye on something while multitasking

## Playback Speed Control

Speed up or slow down any video or audio file:

- **0.25x** — detailed analysis of fast content
- **0.5x, 0.75x** — comfortable slow playback
- **1.0x** — normal speed
- **1.25x, 1.5x** — faster listening for podcasts and lectures
- **2.0x** — maximum speed for power users

## Subtitle Support

AV Player handles all common subtitle formats:

- **SRT** — the most widely supported format
- **SSA / ASS** — styled subtitles with fonts and positioning
- **VTT** — web-native subtitle format

Subtitles load automatically if they're in the same folder as your video with a matching filename. You can also load subtitle files manually.

## Smart Media Manager

AV Player scans and organizes your entire media library:

- **Grid and list views** — choose your preferred browsing style
- **Sort by name, date, size, or duration**
- **Quick search** across all videos and audio files
- **Recently played** section — jump back to where you left off
- **Recently added** — new files appear immediately after download
- **Hide private folders** — keep personal content out of the main library
- **Call recording management** — organize recordings in one place
